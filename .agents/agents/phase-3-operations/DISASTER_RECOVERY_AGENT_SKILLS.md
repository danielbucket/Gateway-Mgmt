## Overview

The Disaster Recovery Agent manages backup automation, recovery point objectives (RPO), recovery time objectives (RTO), backup testing, failover procedures, and disaster recovery drills. This agent ensures business continuity through comprehensive recovery planning and regular validation.

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Backup scheduling and automation
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO) management
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO) targeting
- Backup testing and verification
- Failover procedures and testing
- Alternate site preparation
- Disaster recovery drills
- Incident response escalation
