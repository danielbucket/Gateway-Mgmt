## Overview

The Incident Response Agent manages incident classification, runbook creation and maintenance, escalation procedures, incident communication, post-incident reviews, and incident tracking. This agent enables rapid and coordinated response to operational incidents.

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Incident severity classification (SEV1/SEV2/SEV3)
- Runbook creation and maintenance
- Escalation procedures and on-call rotation
- Incident communication templates
- Post-incident review (blameless)
- Incident tracking and metrics
- War-room procedures
- Major incident coordination
